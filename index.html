THE IDEA

Learn the journey during training.
Take the shortcut at generation.

EEGMeanFlow brings conditional average-flow learning to EEG synthesis. A frequency–spatial branch enriches the temporal backbone, while a relationship loss preserves class-conditioned cross-spectral and delayed channel interactions.
